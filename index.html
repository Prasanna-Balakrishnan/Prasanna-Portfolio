PUBLICATIONS

Towards Sustainable Charcoal Production from Prosopis Juliflora: Integrating Local Knowledge and Clean Technology

ICSRF'25 · Nov 20, 2025

This study examines charcoal production from Prosopis juliflora as a key livelihood source in rural Tamil Nadu, particularly in Adancheri village. It highlights the environmental, health, and socioeconomic challenges associated with traditional earth mound kiln practices, including low yield efficiency and high greenhouse gas emissions.

Using Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA) tools such as resource mapping, seasonal calendars, and stakeholder consultations, the research identifies gaps in existing production systems. The study proposes improved kiln technologies, capacity-building initiatives, and sustainable resource management strategies tailored to local conditions. The findings emphasize PRA as an effective, community-centered approach for enabling safer and environmentally sustainable charcoal production systems.
